Build a tiny tool
for the dental chair
in 7 days.

A concept for a week-long jam where devs, dental pros, and students ship small browser tools: explainers, games, calculators. Teams of 1 to 3. Entries have to run in a browser and be free.

Sample countdown to a hypothetical start date. No event is scheduled.

Rules (draft)

⏱ 7 days

Start from scratch during the jam. Libraries are fine.

🌐 Runs in a browser

One link and no install. A free tier must exist.

🔒 No patient data

Use sample data only, and nothing that diagnoses.

🧑‍⚕️ Ask a pro

Bonus points for showing it to a real clinician and sharing what they said.

📣 Build in public

Post your progress. The best log wins a prize.

⚖️ Judging

Usefulness 40% · polish 30% · fun 20% · log 10%.
