Line the sliding slab up with the tower. Only the overlap stays, and a clean miss ends the run. Amber embers show up every few floors.

  • Space, click, or Drop sets the slab down. On a touch screen, tap the kiln or the Drop button.
  • The slab slides side to side, then front to back. The pale mark shows where it will land.
  • Hang-off glass is cut away. Land almost flush and the tower keeps its full size.
  • Cover an amber ember with the slab to catch it.
