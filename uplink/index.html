Relay Shaft

A dark 3D service shaft under a dead relay. You are the courier lamp. Jump the ledges and climb the stair before the drop takes you back to the floor.

  • AD or the arrow keys move along the shaft.
  • Space, W, or Up jumps. Tap for a short hop, hold for the full arc.
  • Missing a ledge drops you back to the floor. The shaft above is still dark — this stair is the way in.
