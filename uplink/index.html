Relay Shaft

A dark 3D service shaft under a dead relay. Jump the ledges, pocket the blue cores, and land on the green pad at the top.

  • AD or the arrow keys move. Space, W, or Up jumps. Hold for the full arc.
  • Each meter is 10 points. Each blue core is 100. Green chevrons launch you. Red lips crumble. Blue slabs slide.
  • Miss a ledge and the lamp drops. R starts another shaft. Stand on the relay pad to light it.
