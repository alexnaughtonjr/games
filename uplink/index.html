Uplink

Relay Shaft

A dark 3D service shaft under a dead relay. The courier lamp lights the ledges. Jump, pocket the blue cores, and land on the green pad at the top.

  • AD or the arrow keys move. Space, W, or Up jumps. Hold for the full arc.
  • Each meter is 10 points. Each blue core is 100. Green chevrons launch you. Red lips crumble. Blue slabs slide.
  • Land on a blue beacon and a later fall puts you back there. Red sweep beams count as a fall too.
  • With no beacon yet, a miss ends the run. R starts another shaft. The dish at the top is the relay.
  • P or Esc pauses. On a touch screen, use the buttons along the bottom.
