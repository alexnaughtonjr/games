A 3D music stage that never ends,
and your chat directs it.

Play your own music (mic, line-in or a track) or let the built-in groove machine jam. Viewers type a vibe like "neon rain over tokyo", and the stage changes its colors, motion, particles and even the groove to match. Every few segments there's a short sponsor break.

▶ Open the stage

🎛 Built-in groove

A generative beat made from pure Web Audio oscillators. No samples, no licensing. Prompts change its key, tempo and density.

💬 Prompt queue

Each prompt airs for 20 seconds. Words it doesn't know still get a unique look generated from the text, so every prompt does something.

📺 Stream mode

Press H to hide the UI, then capture the window in OBS for X, IG or FB lives.

Honest note: there's no AI video here. A rule-based "director" turns words into visuals, so it costs $0 to run and can stay up forever. Prompts are typed on the stage computer for now; remote chat input is on the board below.
