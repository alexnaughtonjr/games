A 2-minute brushing game
kids actually want to play.

Tooth Defender is a 3D game where kids scrub plaque critters off a row of teeth. One round lasts exactly two minutes, and a glowing corner moves around the mouth every 30 seconds, the same pattern a proper brush should follow. Finishing earns a printable "Brushing Champion" certificate.

⏱ Teaches the habit

Two minutes, four zones, 30 seconds each. The game is the timer.
